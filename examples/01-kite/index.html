How a day runs

A list tells you what's left. A course tells you what's next, how long it should take, and what to do when a meeting eats your afternoon.

Kite, the basics4 controls

Start

Kite opens on today's first leg, not on everything you haven't done.

1

Plan the course

Drag tasks into the order you'll run them. Each leg gets a time estimate based on how long similar tasks took you before.

2

Punch each control

One tap marks a task done and starts the clock on the next leg. No timers to remember.

3

Take a different route

Meeting ran over? Kite re-plans the legs you have left and tells you what no longer fits.

4

Read your splits

Planned against actual for every leg, so tomorrow's estimates start matching reality.

Splits, not guilt

At the end of the day Kite shows where time actually went. After a week of splits, your plans stop being wishful.
